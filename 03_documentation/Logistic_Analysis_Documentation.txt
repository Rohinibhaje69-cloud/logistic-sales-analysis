# Logistics Sales Analysis 

---

## 1. Project Overview

The Logistics Analytics Dashboard is a business-intelligence reporting solution designed to consolidate logistics performance indicators into a single interactive management view.

The dashboard focuses on four primary KPI areas:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Shipment volume
- Delivery performance

It also provides analysis across region, product category, and employee.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The project addresses a common reporting problem: operational data is available, but decision-makers need a faster way to understand performance.

The dashboard is intended to reduce manual analysis and provide a structured way to identify:

- High-performing regions
- High-performing product categories
- Employee performance
- Shipment activity
- Delivery efficiency
- Areas requiring further investigation

## 7. Analytical Method

A recommended analytical workflow is:

1. Import the logistics dataset.
2. Validate field names and data types.
3. Remove duplicates.
4. Handle missing values.
5. Validate revenue and profit fields.
6. Validate shipment identifiers.
7. Standardize region/category/employee names.
8. Calculate KPIs.
9. Build summary tables.
10. Create charts.
11. Add interactive filters.
12. Validate dashboard totals against source data.
13. Document business insights.

---

## 8. Quality Controls

Recommended validation checks:

- Source revenue = dashboard revenue
- Source profit = dashboard profit
- Source shipment count = dashboard shipment count
- Average delivery calculation uses valid delivery records
- Category totals reconcile with overall totals
- Region totals reconcile with overall totals
- Employee totals reconcile with overall totals

---

## 9. Recommended Advanced Metrics

For a production-grade logistics dashboard:

### Profit Margin
`Profit Margin % = Total Profit / Total Revenue × 100`

### Revenue per Shipment
`Revenue per Shipment = Total Revenue / Total Shipments`

### Profit per Shipment
`Profit per Shipment = Total Profit / Total Shipments`

### On-Time Delivery %
`On-Time Delivery % = On-Time Shipments / Total Shipments × 100`

### Delivery Delay
`Delivery Delay = Actual Delivery Days - Target Delivery Days`
